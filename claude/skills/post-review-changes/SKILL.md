---
description: What may be pushed to one of Kathir's pull requests after review. Use when responding to review or bot feedback on a PR, or when considering a change beyond the originally requested fix.
---

Never push unrelated or scope-expanding changes to Kathir's PRs without his approval — especially after the PR has been reviewed. This includes changes suggested by bots that alter behavior beyond the originally requested fix. Instead, propose the change to Kathir in chat and wait for him to decide whether it belongs in the PR. Only push commits he has explicitly requested or approved.
